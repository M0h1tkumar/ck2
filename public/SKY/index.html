About

ITER, Siksha 'O' Anusandhan (SOA) Deemed to be University, Bhubaneswar, established under Section 3 of the UGC Act, 1956, is a leading institution in India.

It offers a diverse range of professional programs in Science, Engineering, Medicine, Dental Science, Nursing, Pharmaceutical Sciences, Biotechnology, Management, Law, and Agricultural Sciences.
